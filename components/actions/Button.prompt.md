Call-to-action button — flat fill, 2px border, square corners, body font 600.
```jsx
<Button>Book a demo</Button>
<Button variant="accent" size="lg">Get the report</Button>
<Button variant="inverse">Read more</Button>
```
- Variants: primary, secondary (outline), accent / accent-2 (neutral-900 text; counts as the layout's one accent), inverse (on primary), ghost.
- Hover inverts fill/text; press nudges 1px down. Disabled = neutral-200 fill.
