Flat content block for grouping a title, body and footer — hairline border or solid brand fill, never a shadow.
```jsx
<Card eyebrow="Case study" title="Fewer tools, faster answers" footer={<KeyNumber value="−62%" tone="accent-1" size={32} />}>
  One team replaced four dashboards with a single view.
</Card>
```
- Tones: default, inverse (primary), support (secondary).
