Headlines — display (one per layout), h1 (page/slide title), h2 (section/block title). Sentence case, left-aligned.
```jsx
<Heading level="display" highlight="decisions easier">Data that makes decisions easier</Heading>
<Heading level="h2">What changed this quarter</Heading>
```
- `highlight` colors one phrase in primary. `onInverse` for white on primary.
