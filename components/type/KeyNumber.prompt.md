Big metric in the number role — heading 700, lh 1.0, −2% tracking — with a caption label.
```jsx
<KeyNumber value="12.4M" label="Active users" />
<KeyNumber value="+38%" tone="accent-1" label="Year over year" />
```
- Accent tones are the only place accent colors may appear as text; size is clamped to the contrast minimums. `onInverse` forces white.
